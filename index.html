Case study

A wedding where every guest plays

For a wedding with 100+ guests, we built a party web app that guests open by scanning a QR code. No install, no sign-up.

  1. Stamp rallyFour activities, four stamps — finishing them all unlocks a bonus.
  2. Unity runner gameA WebGL game with a live leaderboard; the top three go on stage.
  3. Fair prize card drawOnly three rare cards for the whole hall, with equal odds for every guest and no double winners.
  4. Live wishesMessages fly across every phone as bullet comments the moment they are sent.
  5. Photo prints by mailGuests pick a photo; it is saved to Google Drive, printed and mailed after the wedding.
  6. Admin consoleThe couple see who is online, manage prizes and fix data — protected by Google sign-in.

FAQ

Questions we often get

How long does a project take?

A wedding or event app usually takes 4–8 weeks. Larger apps are planned in stages. Please contact us early if you have a fixed date.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the features. After a free consultation we send a fixed quote, so there are no surprises.

Can you work with clients outside Japan?

Yes. We work remotely in English, Japanese or Chinese, with clients in Japan, Hong Kong, Taiwan and beyond.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. Event apps run in the phone's browser — guests just scan a QR code.
